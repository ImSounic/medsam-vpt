\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:limits}

All models are adapted on dermoscopy, so we have not shown that the anchors help when a far-OOD set is the training domain. The drift curves and the full-data budget point are single-seed, and far-OOD variance on mammography is large for every method. The CKA loss adds about 30 percent to training time; the drift signal needs a base-model forward and is weak on DMID; the regularised model inherits the base's sensitivity to loose boxes in-domain.

Within those bounds, far-OOD failure of adapted MedSAM arises where the mask decoder's representations leave the base model's, when encoder and decoder adapt together without an anchor; a decoder anchor removes much of the failure at no in-domain cost, an encoder anchor alone makes it worse, and for LoRA the size of the departure on a single image is a label-free failure signal that holds on a domain used for nothing else.
