\paragraph{Drift ranks LoRA's failures.}
Over three seeds LoRA fails on 61 to 82 of 647 BUSI images and 130 to 173 of 362 CBIS-DDSM images, and drift ranks these failures with AUROC $0.96 \pm 0.01$ and $0.80 \pm 0.02$. On DMID (113 to 175 failures of 269) drift scores $0.69 \pm 0.11$. The result does not hinge on the failure threshold (seed 0: AUROC 0.90 to 0.97 on BUSI and 0.73 to 0.82 on CBIS-DDSM for thresholds from 0.3 to 0.7; supplement). Ranking quality translates into a review budget (seed 0): flagging the 20 percent of BUSI images with the highest drift captures 87 percent of LoRA's failures and leaves a failure rate of 1.5 percent among the unflagged, from 9.4 percent overall; on CBIS-DDSM (42 percent failures) flagging half captures 79 percent (supplement). LoRA's IoU estimate is at chance on all three sets ($0.55 \pm 0.16$, $0.51 \pm 0.09$, $0.57 \pm 0.16$): after adaptation it collapses to a band between 0.4 and 0.6 regardless of mask quality.

\paragraph{VPT: strong on the ladder, weak on DMID.}
VPT-shallow and VPT-deep failures are ranked by drift with AUROC $0.95 \pm 0.01$ and $0.92 \pm 0.01$ on BUSI and $0.72 \pm 0.03$ and $0.69 \pm 0.03$ on CBIS-DDSM, but on DMID only $0.57 \pm 0.09$ and $0.55 \pm 0.09$, with one seed of each at chance, although the per-image spread of drift there is as wide as on CBIS-DDSM. Drift is therefore not a universal signal: reliable for LoRA on the ladder, weaker and seed-dependent on DMID, weak for VPT there.

\paragraph{The IoU estimate reacts to the prompt, not to the modality.}
Full fine-tuning and encoder-only LoRA fail on only 2 to 14 ladder images at tight boxes, too few to score, and full fine-tuning's 50 to 52 DMID failures are missed by both signals in every seed (drift $0.57 \pm 0.01$, IoU estimate $0.32 \pm 0.03$, below chance; encoder-only LoRA: 0.69 and 0.24). Under 50\,px jitter (seed 0) their 220 to 236 CBIS-DDSM failures are detected by the IoU estimate with AUROC 0.75 to 0.79 while drift is below chance (0.23 to 0.36). The IoU head flags a poor box, which every model can sense, rather than a modality it has never seen. Drift marks predictions on which the adapted decoder has left the region the base model would use; it is uninformative once the decoder is retrained wholesale and moves for every image, or is held in place by the CKA anchor (0.35 and 0.46 on DMID). Neither signal is a self-assessment: drift needs a base-model forward, and both were validated against ground truth offline.
