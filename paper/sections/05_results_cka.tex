\paragraph{Multi-seed effect.}
Table~\ref{tab:cka} gives the main comparison. The decoder CKA loss leaves in-domain and near-OOD Dice within 0.003 and raises far-OOD Dice by 0.051 on BUSI and 0.104 on CBIS-DDSM, with HD95 falling by 96 and 152 pixels: the regulariser removes the boundary failures of unregularised LoRA rather than small overlap losses. The BUSI gain is stable across seeds (std 0.008 against 0.032 without the loss); on CBIS-DDSM the seed spread is large for both models (std 0.095) but the gain holds in every seed, and the paired test is significant on all four datasets. With tight-box training the loss gives smaller same-signed gains (0.033, 0.027; seed 0); with rand100 training it hurts (0.059 and 0.032 lower), so the two regularisers compound at mild jitter and conflict under heavy jitter.

\paragraph{Prompt robustness.}
In Fig.~\ref{fig:curves} the regularised pm20 model is the best of the six on both far-OOD sets at every expansion, with an advantage that narrows as boxes loosen: the paired gain on BUSI falls from 0.053 at 20\,px to 0.017 at 200\,px, and on CBIS-DDSM from 0.088 to not significant (per-level tests in the supplement). On the dermoscopy sets the sign flips beyond 50\,px (0.033 lower at 200\,px, $p < 10^{-20}$): anchoring to a base trained with tight boxes inherits its sensitivity to loose boxes in-domain. The rand100 regime buys in-domain prompt robustness at a far-OOD price that the CKA loss makes worse.

\paragraph{Where the anchor must be.}
The hook ablation (LoRA, 20\,px training, tight-box evaluation, three seeds; figure in the supplement) separates the two halves of the model. With the loss on encoder activations alone, far-OOD Dice falls below the unregularised baseline in every seed, to $0.725 \pm 0.023$ on BUSI and $0.357 \pm 0.059$ on CBIS-DDSM (paired per-image loss 0.037 and 0.055, $p < 10^{-4}$), at unchanged in-domain Dice. Decoder hooks give the gains of Table~\ref{tab:cka}. Anchoring both is best: $0.834 \pm 0.027$ and $0.626 \pm 0.098$, a paired gain over decoder hooks of 0.010 and 0.088 ($p < 10^{-4}$) and over no regulariser of 0.062 and 0.175, for a 0.003 in-domain cost. The seed spread on CBIS-DDSM is large for every arm (two trainings of one configuration reached 0.536 and 0.779), so we report paired statistics rather than best seeds. The decoder is the locus, and the encoder anchor helps only once the decoder is anchored too.

\paragraph{A second mammography source.}
On DMID, which no model or probe has seen (269 images, tight boxes, table in the supplement), every comparison matches CBIS-DDSM: LoRA falls from a zero-shot 0.637 to 0.402, decoder-only, encoder-only and full fine-tuning reach 0.73, the decoder anchor lifts LoRA to 0.697 and encoder hooks alone give 0.366.
