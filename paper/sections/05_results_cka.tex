\paragraph{Decoder anchoring repairs LoRA.}
Table~\ref{tab:cka} answers RQ1. The decoder CKA loss leaves in-domain and near-OOD Dice within 0.003 of plain LoRA and raises far-OOD Dice by 0.051 on BUSI and 0.104 on CBIS-DDSM, with HD95 falling by 96 and 152 pixels: it removes LoRA's boundary failures rather than small overlap losses. A paired Wilcoxon test over per-image Dice (20\,px evaluation jitter, Holm-corrected) is significant on all four datasets ($p < 0.001$). The BUSI gain is stable across seeds; on CBIS-DDSM the seed spread is large for every LoRA variant (two trainings of one configuration reached 0.536 and 0.779), but the gain holds in every seed. With tight-box training the loss gives smaller same-signed gains (0.033 and 0.027, seed 0); with heavy jitter up to 100\,px it hurts (0.059 and 0.032 lower). Under looser evaluation boxes the far-OOD gain narrows (BUSI: 0.053 at 20\,px, 0.017 at 200\,px) and in-domain Dice drops slightly below plain LoRA beyond 50\,px (supplement).

\paragraph{The decoder is the place to anchor.}
Anchoring the encoder alone lowers far-OOD Dice below plain LoRA in every seed (paired per-image loss 0.037 on BUSI and 0.055 on CBIS-DDSM, $p < 10^{-4}$) at unchanged in-domain Dice. Anchoring both halves is best, a paired gain over decoder hooks of 0.010 and 0.088 ($p < 10^{-4}$). The encoder anchor thus hurts alone and helps only on top of the decoder anchor; one untested reading is that holding encoder features in place pushes the adaptation into the free decoder.

\paragraph{Simpler adaptation is still more robust.}
Decoder-only and full fine-tuning, trained in the same regime, beat every LoRA variant far-OOD (0.88 to 0.90 on BUSI, 0.78 to 0.81 on CBIS-DDSM) with seed spreads below 0.01; full fine-tuning also leads in-domain. The CKA loss is thus a repair for adapters that move encoder and decoder together, and evidence for where their failure arises, not a better adapter. On DMID, which no model or probe has seen (269 images, supplement), every comparison matches CBIS-DDSM: with tight-box training LoRA falls from a zero-shot 0.637 to 0.402, the decoder anchor lifts it to 0.697 and decoder-only, encoder-only and full fine-tuning reach 0.73; with 20\,px training encoder hooks alone give 0.366 against 0.556 for decoder hooks.
